porespy.generators.line_segment
===============================

.. py:function:: porespy.generators.line_segment(X0, X1)

   Calculate the voxel coordinates of a straight line between the two
   given end points

   :param X0 and X1: The [x, y] or [x, y, z] coordinates of the start and end points of
                     the line.
   :type X0 and X1: array_like

   :returns: **coords** -- A list of lists containing the X, Y, and Z coordinates of all
             voxels that should be drawn between the start and end points to
             create a solid line.
   :rtype: list of lists

   .. rubric:: Examples

   `Click here
   <https://porespy.org/examples/generators/reference/line_segment.html>`__
   to view online example.
