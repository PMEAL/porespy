porespy.networks.partition_skeleton
===================================

.. py:function:: porespy.networks.partition_skeleton(sk, juncs, dt)

   Divides skeleton into pore and throat voxels given junctions

   :param sk: A boolean image of the skeleton of the phase of interest
   :type sk: ndarray
   :param juncs: A boolean array the same shape as `sk` with `True` values indicating the
                 junction points at which the skeleton will be divided.
   :type juncs: ndarray
   :param dt: The distance transform of the phase of interest
   :type dt: ndarray

   :returns: **results** -- A `Results` object with images of `pores` and `throats` each containing
             the labelled clusters of connected voxels.
   :rtype: dataclass
