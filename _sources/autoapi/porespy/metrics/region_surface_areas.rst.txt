porespy.metrics.region_surface_areas
====================================

.. py:function:: porespy.metrics.region_surface_areas(regions, voxel_size=(1, 1, 1), strel=None)

   Extract the surface area of each region in a labeled image.

   Optionally, it can also find the the interfacial area between all
   adjoining regions.

   :param regions: An image of the pore space partitioned into individual pore regions.
                   Note that zeros in the image will not be considered for area
                   calculation.
   :type regions: ndarray
   :param voxel_size: The resolution of the image, expressed as the length of one side of a
                      voxel, so the volume of a voxel would be **voxel_size**-cubed.  The
                      default is 1.
   :type voxel_size: tuple
   :param strel: The structuring element used to blur the region.  If not provided,
                 then a spherical element (or disk) with radius 1 is used.  See the
                 docstring for ``mesh_region`` for more details, as this argument is
                 passed to there.
   :type strel: array_like

   :returns: **areas** -- A list containing the surface area of each region, offset by 1, such
             that the surface area of region 1 is stored in element 0 of the list.
   :rtype: list

   .. rubric:: Examples

   `Click here
   <https://porespy.org/examples/metrics/reference/region_surface_areas.html>`__
   to view online example.
