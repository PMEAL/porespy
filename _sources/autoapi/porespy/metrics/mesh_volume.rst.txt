porespy.metrics.mesh_volume
===========================

.. py:function:: porespy.metrics.mesh_volume(region, voxel_size=(1.0, 1.0, 1.0))

   Compute the volume of a single region by meshing it

   :param region: An image with a single region labelled as ``True`` (or > 0)
   :type region: ndarray
   :param voxel_size: The resolution of the image, expressed as the length of one side of a
                      voxel, so the volume of a voxel would be **voxel_size**-cubed.  The
                      default is 1.
   :type voxel_size: tuple

   :returns: **volume** -- The volume of the region computed by applyuing the marching cubes
             algorithm to the region, then finding the mesh volume using the
             ``trimesh`` package.
   :rtype: float

   .. rubric:: Examples

   `Click here
   <https://porespy.org/examples/metrics/reference/mesh_volume.html>`__
   to view online example.
