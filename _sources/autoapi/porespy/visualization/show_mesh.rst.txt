porespy.visualization.show_mesh
===============================

.. py:function:: porespy.visualization.show_mesh(mesh)

   Visualizes the mesh of a region as obtained by `get_mesh` function in the
   `metrics` submodule.

   :param mesh: A mesh returned by `skimage.measure.marching_cubes`
   :type mesh: tuple

   :returns: **fig** -- A handle to a matplotlib 3D axis
   :rtype: Matplotlib figure

   .. rubric:: Examples

   `Click here
   <https://porespy.org/examples/visualization/reference/show_mesh.html>`__
   to view online example.
