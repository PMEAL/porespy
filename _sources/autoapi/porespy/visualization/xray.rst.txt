porespy.visualization.xray
==========================

.. py:function:: porespy.visualization.xray(im, axis=0)

   Simulates an X-ray radiograph looking through the porous material.

   The resulting image is colored according to the amount of attenuation an
   X-ray would experience, so regions with more solid will appear darker.

   :param im: ndarray of the porous material with the solid phase marked as 1 or `True`
   :type im: array_like
   :param axis: Specifes the axis along which the camera will point.
   :type axis: int

   :returns: **image** -- A 2D greyscale image suitable for use in matplotlib's `imshow` function.
   :rtype: ndarray

   .. rubric:: Examples

   `Click here
   <https://porespy.org/examples/visualization/reference/sem.html>`__
   to view online example.
