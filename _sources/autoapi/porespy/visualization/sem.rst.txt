porespy.visualization.sem
=========================

.. py:function:: porespy.visualization.sem(im, axis=0)

   Simulates an SEM image looking into the porous material.

   Features are colored according to their depth into the image, so
   darker features are further away.

   :param im: ndarray of the porous material with the solid phase marked as 1 or
              True
   :type im: array_like
   :param axis: Specifes the axis along which the camera will point.
   :type axis: int

   :returns: **image** -- A 2D greyscale image suitable for use in matplotlib's `imshow`
             function.
   :rtype: ndarray

   .. rubric:: Examples

   `Click here
   <https://porespy.org/examples/visualization/reference/sem.html>`__
   to view online example.
