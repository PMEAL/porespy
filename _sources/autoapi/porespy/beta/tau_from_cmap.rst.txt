porespy.beta.tau_from_cmap
==========================

.. py:function:: porespy.beta.tau_from_cmap(c, im, axis)

   Computes the tortuosity factor from a concentration field.

   :param c: The concentration field
   :type c: ndarray
   :param im: The binary image of the porous medium
   :type im: ndarray
   :param axis: The axis along which tortuosity is computed
   :type axis: int

   :returns: **tau** -- The tortuosity factor along the given axis
   :rtype: float
