porespy.beta.fibers_1D
======================

.. py:function:: porespy.beta.fibers_1D(shape, r, n, overlap=None)

   Generates randomly located fibers which are all oriented in z-direction

   :param shape: The shape of the image to generate.  This must be 3D.
   :type shape: list
   :param r: The radius of the fibers to generate
   :type r: int
   :param n: The number of fibers to draw
   :type n: int

   :returns: **im** -- An `ndarray` of the given shape with fibers indicated by `False` and void
             by `True`.
   :rtype: ndarray

   .. rubric:: Notes

   ..  [1] Tomadakis MM, Sotirchos SV. Effective diffusivities and conductivities
       of random dispersions of nonoverlapping and partially overlapping
       unidirectional fibers. Journal of Chemical Physics. 99, 9820–9827 (1993).
       `doi <https://doi.org/10.1063/1.465464>`_
