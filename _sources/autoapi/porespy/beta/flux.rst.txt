porespy.beta.flux
=================

.. py:function:: porespy.beta.flux(c, axis, k=None)

   Computes the layer-by-layer diffusive flux in a given direction.

   :param c: The concentration field
   :type c: ndarray
   :param axis: The axis along which the flux is computed
   :type axis: int
   :param k: The conductivity field
   :type k: ndarray

   :returns: **J** -- The layer-by-layer flux in the given direction
   :rtype: ndarray
