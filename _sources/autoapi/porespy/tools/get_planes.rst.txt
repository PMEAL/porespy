porespy.tools.get_planes
========================

.. py:function:: porespy.tools.get_planes(im, squeeze=True)

   Extracts three planar images from the volumetric image, one for each
   principle axis.  The planes are taken from the middle of the domain.

   :param im: The volumetric image from which the 3 planar images are to be obtained
   :type im: ndarray
   :param squeeze: If `True` (default) the returned images are 2D (i.e. squeezed).  If
                   `False`, the images are 1 element deep along the axis where the slice
                   was obtained.
   :type squeeze: boolean, optional

   :returns: **planes** -- A list of 2D-images
   :rtype: list

   .. rubric:: Examples

   `Click here
   <https://porespy.org/examples/tools/reference/get_planes.html>`__
   to view online example.
