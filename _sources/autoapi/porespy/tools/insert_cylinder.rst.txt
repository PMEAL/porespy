porespy.tools.insert_cylinder
=============================

.. py:function:: porespy.tools.insert_cylinder(im, xyz0, xyz1, r)

   Inserts a cylinder of given radius onto an image

   :param im: Original voxelated image
   :type im: array_like
   :param xyz0: Voxel coordinates of the two end points of the cylinder
   :type xyz0: 3-by-1 array_like
   :param xyz1: Voxel coordinates of the two end points of the cylinder
   :type xyz1: 3-by-1 array_like
   :param r: Radius of the cylinder
   :type r: int

   :returns: **im** -- Original voxelated image overlayed with the cylinder
   :rtype: ndarray

   .. rubric:: Notes

   This function is only implemented for 3D images

   .. rubric:: Examples

   `Click here
   <https://porespy.org/examples/tools/reference/insert_cylinder.html>`__
   to view online example.
