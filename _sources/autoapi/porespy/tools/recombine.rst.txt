porespy.tools.recombine
=======================

.. py:function:: porespy.tools.recombine(ims, slices, overlap)

   Recombines image blocks back into full image

   :param ims: The block of the original image, which may or may not have been
               processed.
   :type ims: list of ndarrays
   :param slices: The `slice` objects which were used to obtain the chunks in `ims`
   :type slices: list of slice objects
   :param overlap: The amount of overlap used when creating blocks
   :type overlap: int of list ints

   :returns: **im** -- An image constituted from the chunks in ``ims``
   :rtype: ndarray

   .. seealso:: :py:obj:`chunked_func`, :py:obj:`get_slices_grid`

   .. rubric:: Examples

   `Click here
   <https://porespy.org/examples/tools/reference/recombine.html>`__
   to view online example.
