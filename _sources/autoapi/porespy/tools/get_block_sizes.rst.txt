porespy.tools.get_block_sizes
=============================

.. py:function:: porespy.tools.get_block_sizes(im, block_size_range=[10, 100])

   Finds all viable block sizes between lower and upper limits

   :param im: The binary image to analyze with ``True`` indicating phase of interest.
   :type im: np.array
   :param block_size_range: The [lower, upper] range of the desired block sizes. Default is [10, 100]
   :type block_size_range: sequence of 2 ints

   :returns: **sizes** -- All the viable block sizes in the specified range
   :rtype: ndarray

   .. rubric:: Notes

   This is called by `rev_tortuosity` to determine what size blocks to use.
