porespy.tools.ps_round
======================

.. py:function:: porespy.tools.ps_round(r, ndim, smooth=True)

   Creates round structuring element with the given radius and dimensionality

   :param r: The desired radius of the structuring element
   :type r: scalar
   :param ndim: The dimensionality of the element, either 2 or 3.
   :type ndim: int
   :param smooth: Indicates whether the faces of the sphere should have the little
                  nibs (``True``) or not (``False``, default)
   :type smooth: boolean

   :returns: **strel** -- A 3D numpy array of the structuring element
   :rtype: ndarray

   .. rubric:: Examples

   `Click here
   <https://porespy.org/examples/tools/reference/ps_round.html>`__
   to view online example.
