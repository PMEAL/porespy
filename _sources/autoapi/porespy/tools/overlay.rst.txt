porespy.tools.overlay
=====================

.. py:function:: porespy.tools.overlay(im1, im2, c)

   Overlays ``im2`` onto ``im1``, given voxel coords of center of ``im2``
   in ``im1``.

   :param im1: Original voxelated image
   :type im1: ndarray
   :param im2: Template voxelated image
   :type im2: ndarray
   :param c: [x, y, z] coordinates in ``im1`` where ``im2`` will be centered
   :type c: array_like

   :returns: **image** -- A modified version of ``im1``, with ``im2`` overlaid at the specified
             location
   :rtype: ndarray

   .. rubric:: Examples

   `Click here
   <https://porespy.org/examples/tools/reference/overlay.html>`__
   to view online example.
