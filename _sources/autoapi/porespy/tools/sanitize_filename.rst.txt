porespy.tools.sanitize_filename
===============================

.. py:function:: porespy.tools.sanitize_filename(filename, ext, exclude_ext=False)

   Returns a sanitized string in the form of name.extension

   :param filename: Unsanitized filename, could be 'test.vtk' or just 'test'
   :type filename: str
   :param ext: Extension of the file, could be 'vtk'
   :type ext: str
   :param exclude_ext: If True, the returned string doesn't have the extension
   :type exclude_ext: bool

   :returns: **sanitized** -- Sanitized filename in form of name.extension
   :rtype: str
